A research and design project on assistive technology: how people with disabilities actually use their tools, where mainstream design quietly fails them, and what to build instead.

I'm keeping it here because it stopped being coursework. It came back when SmartSyllabus had to meet WCAG and AODA requirements for real, and it shows up in how I read any product now. The people at the edges find the design flaws first, and they find them faster than any internal review will.
