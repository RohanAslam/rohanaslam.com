Six studio presentations, three group documents, and one complaint that came back every single time: nobody knows when it is their turn at office hours.

SmartQueue is the answer to that. Students join a queue, see their position, and get called. The TA sees who is waiting and what they are stuck on. Straightforward product, and most of the work was in the parts that are not the queue.

The design system is the piece I would still defend. I wrote it as Kinetic Editorial: neo brutalism crossed with the spacing logic of a fashion magazine, Space Grotesk throughout, emerald and yellow at full saturation, and a rule banning grey hairline borders in favour of background colour shifts. It is the opposite of the wayfinding system this site runs on, which is the point. I wanted to know whether I could hold one strong opinion consistently across a whole product rather than just pick a palette.

It never got past the studio. The design system outlived the app, and a good half of what I learned writing it ended up in the one behind this site.
