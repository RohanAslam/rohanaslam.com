Six small apps, none of which anyone but me ever used.

A Bankist style banking interface with transfers and a session timer, a PIG dice game, a number guessing game that remembered a high score between rounds, an expense tracker and a vacation tracker in React, and a community portal built in Svelte because I wanted to know what the fuss was about.

They are learning reps and I am not going to pretend otherwise. The useful thing about keeping them visible is that the gap between the number guessing game and the job portal is four years, and that gap is the actual claim this site is making.

The one I would rebuild is the Svelte portal. The idea was right and the data model was not.
