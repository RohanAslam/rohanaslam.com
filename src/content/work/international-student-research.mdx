A research site rather than a research report.

Interviewee journey mapping across arrival, settling in and belonging, the core insights that came out of those conversations, and a process section explaining how they were gathered. All of it laid out as pages someone could actually click through.

Publishing research as a site instead of a deck is the decision worth keeping. A journey map wants to be scrolled, and a PDF makes it a picture of a journey map.
